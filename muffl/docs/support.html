Muffl for Apple TV

Muffl plays films and shows from a Plex server and mutes or skips what each viewer has chosen to filter: language, nudity, sexual content and violence. It is for people invited to its developer's Plex library, which needs to be shared with your Plex account first.

Getting started

  1. Open Muffl on your Apple TV. It shows a code.
  2. On your phone or computer, go to plex.tv/link and enter the code.
  3. Pick who is watching. Each viewer has their own filters.

To see what Muffl does first, choose "Watch a demo" on the sign-in screen.

Common problems

"Nothing is shared with this Plex account." Nobody has shared a Plex library with the account you signed in with. Email us (below) to have it shared, then choose Try again, or sign in with another Plex account.

A film plays without filters. The film may not have been analyzed yet. Email us (below) and we will look at it.
