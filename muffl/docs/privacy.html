Muffl privacy policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Muffl is an Apple TV app that plays films and shows from a Plex server and mutes or skips what each viewer has chosen to filter. It works with one Plex library and one Muffl server, both run by Muffl's developer, for people they have invited. This page explains what Muffl keeps about you and why.

In short

Muffl keeps only what it needs to apply your filters: who you are on Plex and what you have chosen to filter. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

What stays on your Apple TV

Your Plex sign-in, kept in the Apple TV's Keychain; which viewer is watching; and the app's settings. These never leave the Apple TV except to sign in to Plex and the Muffl server.

What the Muffl server keeps

  • Your Plex username and display name, and when you last used Muffl.
  • A one-way scrambled copy (a hash) of your Plex sign-in, so the server can recognize you. Your actual Plex sign-in is not stored there.
  • Your filter choices: which kinds of language, nudity, sexual content and violence to filter, for everything and for particular titles.
  • Server logs, kept for fixing problems. They can include your network address; Plex sign-ins are removed from them.

This is used only to apply your filters and to keep the service working.

Plex

Signing in and playing goes through Plex: plex.tv and the Plex server you watch from. Like any Plex app, Muffl tells that Plex server how far you have watched, so "Continue Watching" works. What Plex collects is covered by Plex's privacy policy.

The demo

"Watch a demo" plays a short film built into the app. It needs no sign-in and sends nothing anywhere.

Children

Muffl is a family filter. If a child has their own Plex Home profile, Muffl keeps that profile's name and filter choices, exactly as it does for anyone else, and nothing more.

Removing your data

Email us (below) and we will delete your profile and filter choices from the Muffl server. To cut off the app's access to your Plex account, remove the Apple TV under Authorized Devices in your Plex account.

Changes
